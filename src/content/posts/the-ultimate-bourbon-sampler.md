My sister-in-law got me a present for the holidays: Drinks by the Dram, Bourbon edition.  Apparently, she ordered this from England, for her husband (my brother) and I.  What a great gift! It technically is a Advent Calendar.  I am not keeping up with daily sampling, but try to sample one each night I can.
I will update this post with my newest samples closest to the top of this post.
Of note:  I just jotted notes of my total impression.  I know I didn't break it down into the nose, mouth, and finish.  I'll defer to the experts for that.  And you'll see:  many of the same flavors describe them all (such as leather and cinnamon).  Perhaps as my palate (or array of adjectives) improve, so will my reviews.   Nonetheless, check back to see all my updated postings!
