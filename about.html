About

Old Fashioned Doctor

I write in the first person because that is how the work arrives: one exam room, one glass, one evening that did not go as planned.

This site is not a clinic, not a CME module, and not a bar manual with a white coat stapled to it. It is a notebook. Some entries are about what happens when a patient and a physician want different things. Some are about why a Sazerac went sideways and how to put it back together. A few are just life — soup on the console, a car that should have been in park.

The old Blogger archive lives here now, cleaned up enough to read, not rewritten. I intend to start posting again.

Feedback, arguments, and bottle recommendations: @OldFashionedDr.
